I did not begin studying pixel interfaces to imitate an old handheld. I began because modern interfaces were becoming tiring: boundaries blurred, decoration softened, and important actions became harder to find.

> A constraint is not a style by itself. It becomes a design language when it helps us make consistent decisions.

## Start with an integer grid

Pixel design forces position and proportion to become explicit. Icons should not rest on half pixels, borders should keep a consistent weight, and spacing should not drift by intuition.

## A limited palette assigns responsibility

Paper and ink support reading, green indicates a positive state, coral marks primary actions, and yellow provides temporary emphasis.

## Feedback should feel like a key press

Pixelium borders describe the relationship between controls and surfaces. Pressed states use position, border, and color together, without decorative animation.
