A note becomes useful only when it can be found again. Each week I group screenshots, sentences, and sketches under a few stable themes.

## Preserve the source

Every note keeps its source and date so it does not become an unverifiable claim a few weeks later.

## Add a next step

Each fragment receives one action: verify, quote, draw, or discard.
