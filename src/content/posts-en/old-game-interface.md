Old games could not rely on vague hierarchy or elaborate animation. Every pixel explained state, and every button press needed visible feedback.

## Clear boundaries

Selected, disabled, and dangerous actions often combined color, shape, and position, so they remained legible even on monochrome displays.

## Feedback stays near the action

State changes happened close to the player's input. The same principle still improves modern tool interfaces.
